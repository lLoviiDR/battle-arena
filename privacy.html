Privacy Policy — Battle Arena Live

Battle Arena Live is an interactive live-stream game for entertainment purposes.

  1. Data we use: We do not collect, store, or share any personal data from viewers. The application only receives real-time public gift events (gift name and value) from TikTok LIVE to display visual effects on screen.
  2. Data we do NOT access: We do not access viewers' profiles, messages, videos, followers, or any private information.
  3. Data storage: Gift events are processed in memory only and are not saved to any database.
  4. Contact: for questions, contact the app owner via the TikTok account.
